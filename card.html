Get a Library Card

Join our library community and unlock access to thousands of books, digital resources, and programs. Borrow books, magazines, and newspapers. Access e-books, audiobooks, and streaming services. Reserve materials online. Attend free workshops, classes, and events.

Requirements

To obtain a library card, you must:

  • Be a resident of Vallejo or Solano County
  • Provide proof of current address (utility bill, lease agreement, or official mail)
  • Present a valid photo ID (driver's license, state ID, passport, or school ID)
  • Be at least 5 years old (minors require parent/guardian signature)
